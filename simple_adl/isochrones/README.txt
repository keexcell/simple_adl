A set of isochrones for different surveys:
    - DES: Bressan et al. 2012 isochrones from the ugali suite.
    - LSST DP0: Marigo et al. 2017 isochrones
    - Euclid: PARSEC v1.2 (from Marigo group) isochrones
    - LSST, Euclid, Roman, GAIA: PARSEC v1.2 (also Marigo group). note: LSST, Euclid in AB mags. Roman, GAIA in Vega mags
